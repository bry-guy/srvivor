How to play

Guess the hidden word in six tries. Use your keyboard or tap the letters below. Press Enter (or the ENTER key below) to submit a full word. If rejected, use backspace to edit it.

  • Green / correct: the letter is in the right spot.
  • Gold / present: the letter is in the word, but elsewhere.
  • Gray / absent: no unused copy of this letter is in the word.

Each incorrect accepted guess extinguishes a torch. Invalid words don't cost a turn. Your game follows you across devices.
